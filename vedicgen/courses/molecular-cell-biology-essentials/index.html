Molecular & Cell Biology Essentials

Strong practical and computational work rests on a clear understanding of how cells work. This course rebuilds that foundation efficiently, with an eye on the experiments and data you will meet later.

Course overview

Molecular & Cell Biology Essentials covers the central dogma in modern detail (replication, transcription, splicing and translation), gene regulation, protein structure and function, cell organisation, signalling and the cell cycle.

Each topic is linked to the methods that study it, from PCR and sequencing to microscopy, so that the course prepares you directly for Vedicgen’s bench and data courses.

Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

  1. Explain replication, transcription, splicing and translation
  2. Describe how genes are regulated in prokaryotes and eukaryotes
  3. Relate protein structure to function
  4. Describe cell organisation, signalling and the cell cycle
  5. Connect biological questions to the techniques used to answer them

Skills you will practise

Central dogmaGene regulationProtein structureCell biologySignallingExperimental reasoning

Eligibility and prerequisites

Who can apply

  • Undergraduate and postgraduate students in any life-science, health-science, agriculture or pharmacy programme
  • Graduates preparing for research positions, higher studies or a move into biotechnology
  • Professionals from adjacent fields (engineering, computing, chemistry) who need a working command of biology

Prerequisites

  • School-level biology and chemistry

Best suited to

  • First-year students and those changing field into biology
  • Engineers and computer scientists entering bioinformatics
  • Graduates refreshing fundamentals before a specialised course

Module-wise syllabus

4 modules. Each pairs instructor-led sessions with a hands-on lab on public data.

Topics

  • DNA structure and genomes
  • Replication and repair
  • Mutation

Topics

  • Transcription and splicing
  • Translation and the genetic code
  • Gene regulation
Lab 2Problem set: predict expression outcomes from regulatory changes.

Topics

  • Protein structure and function
  • Organelles and membranes
  • Transport
Lab 3Case study: relate a disease mutation to protein and cell function.

Topics

  • Receptors and pathways
  • Cell-cycle control
  • Cell death
Lab 4Case study: interpret an experiment on a signalling pathway.

Practical sessions and projects

Capstone project

Final assessment

An open-book assessment that asks you to explain experimental results using molecular and cellular mechanisms.

Assessed on the correctness of the analysis, the quality of the code and the reasoning in the write-up. A pass is required for the certificate.

  1. DNA and replication

    Problem set: trace mutations from DNA to protein consequences.

  2. Gene expression

    Problem set: predict expression outcomes from regulatory changes.

  3. Proteins and cells

    Case study: relate a disease mutation to protein and cell function.

  4. Signalling and the cell cycle

    Case study: interpret an experiment on a signalling pathway.

Core reading

  • Alberts B et al. Molecular Biology of the Cell, 7th edition. W. W. Norton, 2022.
