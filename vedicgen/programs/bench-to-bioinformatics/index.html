The path

4 courses, in order

Isolate RNA, measure expression by qPCR and analyse RNA-seq data, linking bench decisions to data quality. Pace is customisable: fast-track by taking courses back to back, or go part-time and spread them out. You agree the order and pace of courses with your mentor when you join.

  1. 01
    Molecular Biology Techniques: DNA, RNA, PCR & Electrophoresis

    Nucleic-acid isolation, PCR and gel electrophoresis, performed by every participant.

    Beginner · 5 days in the lab · ₹15,499 separately
    Wet lab
  2. 02
    Real-Time PCR (qPCR) & Gene Expression Analysis

    From RNA to cDNA to Ct values and fold change, with MIQE-standard analysis.

    Intermediate · 3 days in the lab · ₹8,499 separately
    Wet lab
  3. 03
    NGS Data Analysis with Galaxy (No Coding)

    Quality control, alignment, variants and RNA-seq in a web platform, with no programming.

    Beginner · up to 4 weeks · ₹8,499 separately
    Dry lab
  4. 04
    RNA-seq Data Analysis

    Bulk RNA-seq end to end: quantification, DESeq2, batch effects and pathway enrichment.

    Intermediate · up to 4 weeks · ₹10,999 separately
    Dry lab
  5. Integrated capstone

    A gene-expression study with matched qPCR and RNA-seq analysis.

Eligibility

Who can apply

  • Students and graduates of B.Sc., M.Sc., B.Tech., M.Tech., B.Pharm., M.Pharm. or a PhD programme in biotechnology, microbiology, biochemistry, molecular biology, genetics, life sciences, pharmacy or allied health sciences
  • Laboratory staff, research assistants and faculty who want structured bench training
  • Participants must be 18 or older and follow the laboratory safety induction on day one

You receive

What is included

  • All 4 courses in their in-person lab + live online format
  • An integrated capstone reviewed by a panel
  • A programme certificate and a certificate for each course, each with a verification ID
  • An adviser who reviews your progress between courses
  • All reagents, consumables and protective equipment for the bench courses
